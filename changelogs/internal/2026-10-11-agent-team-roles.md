# Reusable Agent Teams role templates

- Add **Agent Teams → Role templates** for versioned responsibilities, logical resource requirements, local bindings and execution limits. Saving does not create member chat sessions.
- Preview profile/persona inheritance, role overrides and effective values, with field diagnostics for missing, ambiguous, disabled and incompatible resources. New versions preserve previous definitions and bindings.
- Entry mapping: legacy **Agent Teams → Members** remains available for existing member sessions; reusable definitions use **Agent Teams → Role templates → New role**. Migration and connection to the new runtime are delivered separately.
- Update the REST contract and generated client, plus Chinese and English instructions and actual WebUI screenshots. References: #4, parent specification #2.
